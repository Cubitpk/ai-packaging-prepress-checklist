## Prepress Checklist for Packaging Artwork

| Prepress Check | Requirement for Offset & Digital Packaging Presses | CubitPack Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Flat Structural CAD Dieline** | Artwork must sit on a 1:1 flat vector dieline with separate cut and crease layers | [Interactive Dieline Templates](https://cubitpack.com/tools/dieline-templates) |
| **2. Interior Cavity Sizing (`L × W × D`)** | Measure interior usable product + insert dimensions first | [Box Size Calculator](https://cubitpack.com/tools/box-size-calculator) |
| **3. Color Space Conversion** | Convert RGB screen light to calibrated CMYK or Pantone spot inks | [Custom Printed Boxes](https://cubitpack.com/boxes) |
